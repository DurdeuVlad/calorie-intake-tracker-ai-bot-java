# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, technically rigorous place to collaborate. Participants must be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and protect the privacy of journal users. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sexualised conduct, threats, and publication of private food-journal data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed in `SECURITY.md`. Do not include secrets or private user data. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and applies to project spaces and project-related interactions.
